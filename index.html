    Upload files

    Text files (.txt, .md) or audio (voice memos, .m4a, .mp3). Audio gets transcribed. The date comes from the file name when it has one (like 2025-03-14), otherwise from the file's modified date.

      Import from Obsidian

      On a computer, pick your whole vault folder. On a phone, select notes from the Files app. Your text stays exactly as you wrote it. Dates come from a date or created property, a date in the file name (daily notes), or the file's modified date. Tags and [[links]] are kept. Importing the same notes twice skips the ones already there.

        Lots of old notes? Put them in the import/ folder of your repo and ask Claude Code: "convert everything in import/ into journal entries".

        Words to know

        Names and words the transcriber should spell right, one per line. Whisper listens for them, and the cleanup step fixes near misses (like "Oshur Cone" to "Osher Cohen"). Saved in journal/vocabulary.txt, so every device shares the list.
